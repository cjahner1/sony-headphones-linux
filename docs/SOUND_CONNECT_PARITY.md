# Sound Connect parity and future information architecture

This comparison is based on Sony Sound Connect's Android APK resources and feature classes. It is a multi-device app, so individual options remain capability-gated.

## Recommended navigation

Use four top-level pages instead of continuing to expand the dashboard:

1. **Home** — connection, detailed battery state, current listening mode, and shortcuts.
2. **Sound** — Ambient Sound Control, EQ/Custom EQ, Clear Bass, DSEE, sound-quality/connection mode, and immersive features where supported.
3. **Controls** — Speak-to-Chat, touch assignment, Quick Access, wearing behavior, head gestures, voice guidance, and voice assistant.
4. **Device** — multipoint, firmware/update, charging and battery care, reset, help, and device history.

Each nontrivial control should open a dedicated detail page rather than adding more sliders to Home.

## Current parity

- Battery; volume; ANC/Ambient with level and Focus on Voice
- Speak-to-Chat; DSEE; Clear Bass; voice-guidance volume
- Playback controls and multipoint playback-source handling
- Automatic multipoint switching and XM6 left/right touch assignment

## Next high-value gaps

1. Custom EQ presets and band editor
2. Wearing detection / automatic pause-resume
3. Adaptive Sound Control activity and location profiles
4. Connection mode: sound quality, latency, and LE Audio when supported
5. Voice assistant choice and training
6. Firmware update, reset, battery-care, and charging screens

## Model-conditional / optional

- Find Your Equalizer
- Head-gesture training
- 360 Reality Audio, head tracking, and sound position
- Safe Listening history
